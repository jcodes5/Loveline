# Loveline Design System
Version: 1.0

## Design Philosophy
Loveline should feel:
- Warm
- Personal
- Calm
- Elegant
- Minimal
- Premium but intimate

Core idea:
> "Someone thought about me today."

---

## Visual Identity

### Brand Name
Loveline

### Tagline
A little place for love, every day.
